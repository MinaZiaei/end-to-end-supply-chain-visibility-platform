## End-to-End Supply Chain Visibility Platform
## Overview

This project presents the design and implementation of an end-to-end supply chain visibility solution developed to provide a single source of truth across multiple supply chain functions.

The solution consolidated data from vendors, purchase orders, transportation, distribution centers, inventory, and stores into a centralized reporting environment that enabled stakeholders to monitor performance, identify bottlenecks, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem

Critical supply chain activities were distributed across multiple systems and teams, making it difficult to identify the source of service failures, monitor SLA compliance, and understand the impact of delays on customer delivery performance.

## Objectives

Create an end-to-end visibility framework that tracks operational performance from vendor commitment through final customer delivery, providing stakeholders with a single source of truth for performance monitoring and root-cause analysis.

## Dashboard Design Framework

The reporting solution was developed using a three-tier analytical model to support different stakeholder groups.

## Executive Dashboard

Designed for senior leadership and operational directors.

Provided:

Overall service performance
KPI scorecards
SLA compliance
Cost and operational impact indicators
Regional and division performance summaries

Purpose:

Strategic decision making
Business performance monitoring
Risk identification
## Management Dashboard

Designed for managers and functional leaders.

Provided:

Trend analysis
Vendor performance monitoring
Transportation performance
Distribution center performance
KPI decomposition and bottleneck analysis

Purpose:

Performance management
Operational improvement
Prioritization of corrective actions
## Operational Dashboard

Designed for planners and operational teams.

Provided:

Purchase-order-level visibility
Detailed root cause analysis
Transaction-level investigation

Purpose:

Issue resolution
Daily operations management
Process monitoring
